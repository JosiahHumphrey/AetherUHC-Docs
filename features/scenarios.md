# Scenarios

AetherUHC comes with **139+ fully implemented, highly configurable scenarios** out of the box. Scenarios modify the base rules of Ultra Hardcore to create unique, varied, and exciting gameplay experiences.

## Enabling & Disabling Scenarios

Scenarios can be toggled via the in-game GUI or commands:
- **Command:** `/scenarios` (Opens the Scenario Manager menu)
- **Host Permission required:** `aetheruhc.host`

## Popular Scenarios Included

Here are just a few of the most popular scenarios included in AetherUHC:

### CutClean
Ores drop their smelted counterparts (e.g., Iron Ore drops Iron Ingots). Food drops cooked (e.g., Cows drop Steak). This eliminates the need for furnaces and speeds up the early game.

### Timebomb
When a player dies, their items are placed in a chest rather than dropping on the ground. A countdown timer begins above the chest, and after 30 seconds, the chest explodes, destroying the loot and damaging anyone nearby.

### Timber
Breaking the bottom block of a tree will cause the entire tree to break, dropping all logs and apples instantly.

### Double Ores
All ores mined will drop double the normal yield.

### Fireless
Players cannot take fire or lava damage. This applies in both the Overworld and the Nether.

### Bowless
Bows cannot be crafted or used. String and Feathers cannot be obtained.
